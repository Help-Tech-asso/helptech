2. Services covered

(a) IT assistance on site in Montréal or remotely; (b) creation of simple websites remotely; (c) advice on choosing and purchasing a domain name.

3. Prices and donations

The services are free and require no membership fee. A donation or voluntary contribution may be made, but it never constitutes a mandatory payment in exchange for the service. No donation is required before or during the service. The association only issues a tax receipt if it is authorized to do so. Third-party costs (domain name, hosting, subscriptions) are paid directly by the beneficiary to the chosen provider; the association receives no commission.

Requests are made by email. The association accepts them depending on its availability and may decline them without having to justify itself, in particular if the request falls outside its purpose or is unlawful. An appointment is confirmed in writing.

For in-person assistance, the beneficiary guarantees a safe environment; for remote assistance, remote control is only taken with their explicit consent and may be interrupted by them at any time.

Provide accurate information; never communicate passwords or banking details to the association (it does not ask for them); for a website, hold the rights to the content provided (texts, images, logos); not request any illegal, misleading or hateful content.

The website and the domain name belong to the beneficiary, who receives the access credentials. Unless otherwise stated, the association transfers free of charge the rights to the elements it created. The association may mention the project in its references, unless the beneficiary objects.

8. Right of withdrawal

As the services are free, no payment is due; the beneficiary may cancel an appointment or stop the assistance at any time, free of charge, by informing the association.

The association is only liable for direct damages caused by its proven fault. It is not responsible for losses of data that was not backed up, for failures of third-party providers, or for the use the beneficiary makes of the website. It is advisable to back up your data before any intervention.
